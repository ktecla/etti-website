Who we are

Ten years of
building futures.

For 10 years, Eastman Technical Training Institute has helped students turn ambition into practical ability and practical ability into opportunity.

Our story

A school built around what comes next.

ETTI is a technical and vocational school in Embu, Kenya, committed to making relevant, hands-on education accessible to the next generation of skilled professionals.

Our mission

Equip students to excel in the global market.

To equip students with practical skills, an innovative mindset, and the professional attitude required to excel in the global market.

Our vision

Skilled people. Stronger communities.

To be a leading centre of excellence for practical training, innovation, and professional development in Kenya and beyond.
